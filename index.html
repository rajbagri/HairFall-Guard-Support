Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your scans, routine, and photos.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → HairFall Guard, and choose Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Restore Purchases in the app's paywall if a purchase isn't reflected on a new device.
